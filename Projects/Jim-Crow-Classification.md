## Jim Crow Legal Text Classification

Finetuned DistilBERT model to classify sentences from a large corpus of historical laws. 
The project included corpus construction, preprocessing, supervised model training, evaluation, and error analysis.

**Role:** Machine-learning and Technical lead  
**Tools:** Python, Hugging Face Transformers, Tensorflow, pandas  
**Methods:** Transformer classification, sentence segmentation, batch training, validation  
**Result:** High-performing classifiers for identifying discriminatory legal provisions
